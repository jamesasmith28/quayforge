North Pole or Bust! privacy notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Applies to the v0.52.1 test release.

This notice describes the release candidate. Optional test adverts are off by default; live ads are blocked. Public release settings and operator details are still being verified. We will update this notice before public distribution.

Who we are

QuayForge Studios is the UK-based independent developer of North Pole or Bust! and the contact for questions about personal data. Email quayforge@gmail.com for privacy and support requests.

What stays on your device

Campaign progress, your kart name, earned Scrap, upgrades, selected cosmetics, challenge attempts, settings (including your optional age group and advertising preference), and unsent feedback drafts are stored locally. There is no player login or cloud save service in this build. Your operating system may back up app data according to your settings. Removing the app or clearing app data may remove progress. Restoring Apple purchases does not restore campaign progress.

Optional feedback and support

In-game feedback is available only if you select the 18 or over age group. If you choose to send feedback, we receive the report category, message, game version, platform, a pseudonymous Firebase user ID, report status and server timestamp. Optional diagnostics add the expedition, zone, screen dimensions and device/browser user-agent information. Google Firebase Authentication creates a pseudonymous session to submit reports; session tokens are stored on your device. Reports are stored in Cloud Firestore and may be copied to our private issue-tracking records, including Google Sheets. We use reports to investigate problems and improve the game. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you provide. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Rewarded adverts and privacy choices

Watching adverts is optional. The iOS app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform. Google may process IP address, approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention; the exact data depends on permissions and configuration. In this release candidate, optional test adverts are off by default. Players who select the 18 or over age group may enable test adverts in Settings. Players under 18 or who prefer not to say cannot request adverts. The game checks Google’s consent status before requesting ads and asks for non-personalised ads, a general-audience content limit and child-directed ad treatment as an additional precaution. No live advertising is requested by this release candidate. The game does not request Apple tracking permission. Google may still process data for consent, ad delivery, measurement or security. Where the SDK offers a privacy form, adult users can manage their choices from Settings → Support & privacy → Advertising privacy choices. Turn optional adverts off at any time to prevent new ad requests; the game remains playable. See Google's privacy policy and Google's explanation of partner app data.

Premium kart purchases

Apple processes optional non-consumable purchases and payment details through the App Store. The game reads product prices, verified transactions and purchase entitlements to unlock or restore karts. We do not ask for payment card details or send purchase receipts to a QuayForge server in this build. Apple may retain transaction records under its own policy. See Apple's privacy policy.

Why we use information

Our proposed UK GDPR bases are consent for optional advertising-related processing where required; legitimate interests in troubleshooting, security and responding to enquiries (subject to balancing your rights); performance of the purchase contract for delivering paid items; and legal obligations where applicable. Optional diagnostics are provided by your choice. You may withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing.

Sharing and international processing

Google provides advertising, Firebase, Sheets and Gmail services; Apple provides purchases and app distribution. These providers may process data in countries outside the UK. We are confirming the applicable service terms, transfer mechanisms and safeguards before public release. We do not offer a QuayForge player-data marketplace or sell player lists. Advertising partners' own processing is described in their privacy notices.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall, subject to device backups. For launch we propose retaining raw feedback for no more than 90 days and support correspondence for no more than 12 months after resolution, except where needed for legal or security reasons. These periods are not yet enforced on the test backend. You can already request deletion of submitted reports and copies by contacting us; include the feedback reference shown in Settings → Support & privacy if available, and enough information to identify the report. Do not send session tokens or passwords. We may need to verify the request. Apple and Google may retain their own records independently.

Children and families

We do not ask for a birth date, identity document or real name. An optional age-group choice (under 18, 18 or over, or prefer not to say) stays on the device; it is not included in feedback or sent to advertising partners by the game. It is a self-declaration, not age verification. Unknown and under-18 choices disable in-game feedback and advertising. Parents or guardians may contact support for help or about a child’s information. Do not submit personal information in a kart name or feedback. The public-release audience assessment, age assurance, children’s privacy and SDK checks are still outstanding.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent where it is the basis for processing. Email quayforge@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. We will respond within applicable statutory time limits.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when processing changes. The date at the top identifies the current version.
