North Pole or Bust! privacy notice

Draft dated 26 November 2026

This notice describes the current development build and planned release. Advertising and purchase settings, retention periods and the operator's formal details must be confirmed before public release. This is not the final production privacy policy.

Who operates the game?

QuayForge Studios is the working studio name. For privacy enquiries, email quayforge@gmail.com.

Progress and preferences

The game stores campaign progress, earned Scrap, upgrades, selected cosmetics, challenge activity and preferences on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove local progress. Apple purchase entitlements are handled separately.

Optional feedback

If you submit an in-game report, the game sends your selected category, message, app version, platform, a pseudonymous Firebase user ID, and a timestamp and status to Google Firebase/Firestore. If you opt into diagnostics, it may also send details such as your screen dimensions, device/browser information and game progress context. An anonymous Firebase session is used to submit reports. Please avoid including personal or sensitive details in feedback. Reports are used to investigate issues and improve the game.

Rewarded advertising

The iOS build integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded adverts. The SDK may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, depending on configuration and permissions. The development build uses test adverts. Production advertising will use applicable consent controls; where available, use Settings → Advertising privacy choices to manage those choices. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Premium kart purchases

Apple handles checkout through StoreKit. The game receives purchase and entitlement information to unlock and restore premium cosmetics. It does not request your card details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Support and data rights

If you contact us, we process the information you provide to respond and investigate. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email us with your request. You may also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Before launch

We will confirm applicable legal bases, retention and deletion arrangements (including feedback records), service providers, international transfer safeguards, children's privacy and final advertising settings before the game is released.
