North Pole or Bust!

How do I restore a premium kart?

In the iOS release candidate, use Restore Purchases in the game's shop and sign in with the Apple Account used to buy the kart. Purchases and adverts are still being tested.

Why can't I watch a rewarded advert?

Ad availability depends on your connection, privacy choices and the advertising service. If no advert is available, try again later; you can continue to play without watching one.

What if I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting; contact us first. Restoring purchases is not the same as restoring campaign progress.
