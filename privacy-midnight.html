Midnight Merge privacy notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Midnight Merge is not yet released. This notice covers development enquiries, not a final production app. We will publish an app-specific policy before public distribution after checking the final save, advertising, purchases, leaderboard and any other network features.

Who we are

QuayForge Studios develops Midnight Merge. For privacy questions or feedback, email quayforge@gmail.com.

Development enquiries

If you contact us, we receive your email address and information you choose to provide. We use it to respond and investigate issues, based on our legitimate interests in supporting development, subject to your rights. Google Gmail provides our email service and may process information internationally. Do not send sensitive or payment information. We keep messages while needed to respond and resolve the issue; the proposed studio retention schedule is 12 months after resolution, subject to legal or security needs, and will be confirmed before launch.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Contact our email above. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
